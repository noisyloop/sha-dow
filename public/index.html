    How it works

    • Pick an emoji. One per person, one per network.
    • Every post is encoded to binary before it leaves your device.
    • Press and hold a post to peek at it on your screen only.
    • Plain text only. No links, code or pre-encoded blobs.
    • 100 posts per day.
    • Everything is deleted after 24 hours.

    Experimental chat. sha-dow is an experiment provided as-is, with no warranty. Binary is an encoding, not encryption: anyone can decode posts, so don't share anything sensitive.     About sha-dow

    A 24-hour public room where every post is shown as binary. No accounts, no email, no phone number: your identity is an emoji.

    Inspired by Shadow, a research protocol with no phone number and no central identity. Planned next: end-to-end encrypted 1:1 rooms.
